Why Quit Smoking?

20 Minutes

After quitting, your heart rate and blood pressure drop to normal levels.

1-9 Months

Coughing and shortness of breath decrease; lung function improves.

1 Year

Your risk of coronary heart disease is cut in half compared to smokers.

The Hidden Costs of Smoking

  • Smoking causes more than 480,000 deaths per year in the United States
  • Smokers spend an average of $2,000+ per year on cigarettes
  • Smoking damages nearly every organ in your body
  • Secondhand smoke exposure causes nearly 41,000 deaths annually

Stories of Change

Michael, 32

Smoke-free for 2 years

"After smoking for 15 years, I finally quit when my daughter was born. The first month was tough, but now I can run with my kid without getting winded. My sense of taste and smell returned, and I've saved enough money to take my family on vacation."

Sarah, 28

Smoke-free for 1 year

"I started smoking in college due to stress. After multiple failed attempts, I finally quit using nicotine patches and support from friends. My anxiety has decreased, my skin looks better, and I no longer feel controlled by cigarettes."

David, 45

Smoke-free for 5 years

"After a health scare, I quit cold turkey. It was the hardest thing I've ever done, but also the most rewarding. I've regained my stamina, and my doctor says my lung function has improved dramatically. I wish I'd done it sooner."
